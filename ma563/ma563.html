MA563: Introduction to Differential Geometry.

Fall 2026

This is a proof-based introduction to differential geometry, beginning with curves and surfaces in Euclidean space and continuing to intrinsic geometry and Riemannian manifolds. A syllabus can be found here.

The course text is Wolfgang Kühnel, Differential Geometry: Curves–Surfaces–Manifolds, third edition.
